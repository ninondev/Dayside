Bundled dataset: the city index (cities.ttcity) is derived from GeoNames
(https://www.geonames.org), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The data has been transformed
(selection, transliteration checks, and a compact binary encoding) and is
distributed under the same license; GeoNames is not affiliated with and does not
endorse this application. As with the upstream data, it is provided "as is",
without warranty of accuracy, timeliness or completeness. City coordinates for the 312 time zone reference
cities (tzcoords.json) and RustCore/data/country_zones.tsv come from the IANA time zone
database's zone.tab. RustCore/data/country_names.tsv uses macOS Foundation region names;
RustCore/src/day_words.rs adapts Unicode CLDR day-period rules and names for standalone
clock labels. The Unicode License v3 text is retained below.
Place names in Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Russian, German,
Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Vietnamese and Indonesian
are completed and corrected with Wikidata
(https://www.wikidata.org) labels, matched by GeoNames ID (P1566) or by name and
coordinates (Tools/wikidata_labels.py; the tables are RustCore/data/*_names_wikidata.tsv);
Wikidata's structured data is published under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Chinese names for 348 first-level
administrative regions were translated for this project (partly by machine translation)
and are marked as such in RustCore/data/admin1_zh_supplement.tsv.

The shaded relief behind the world map (land, sea and terrain) is derived from Natural Earth
"Gray Earth with Shaded Relief, Hypsography, and Ocean Bottom" 1:50m (https://www.naturalearthdata.com),
which is in the public domain; it was cropped to 80°N–58°S and resized by Tools/make_relief.swift.

The brush-calligraphy column of the epigraph 天涯共此时 (the Assets.xcassets image EpigraphZh) is
artwork made from the glyph outlines of Liu Jian Mao Cao (Copyright 2018 The Liu Jian Mao Cao Project
Authors, https://github.com/googlefonts/liujianmaocao; designers Liu Zhengjiang, Kimberly Geswein and
ZhongQi), which is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
The font itself is not included; five glyph outlines were arranged into one vertical column by
Tools/make_epigraph.swift. The epigraph lines are 张九龄《望月怀远》 (Zhang Jiuling, Tang dynasty) and
"It is always sunrise somewhere." from John Muir, John of the Mountains (1938).
