Dayside for macOS (Intel)

Dayside is a menu bar world clock. This disk image contains the x86_64 build only
and needs macOS 26 or later. It has been checked under Rosetta on Apple
silicon, not on an Intel Mac. The version number is shown in the app under
Settings > Help.

A Mac with Apple silicon must use the Apple silicon build. If you open
this Intel build there, macOS may warn that support for Intel apps is ending.

Install: drag Dayside.app to Applications.

This build is signed ad hoc and is not notarized, so macOS blocks it the first
time. To open it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to the
bottom, click "Open Anyway" and confirm with your password.

Dayside keeps its data on this Mac. It has no telemetry and no account.
Calendars and contacts are read only after you allow access.

Dayside（Intel 版）

菜单栏世界时钟。这个磁盘映像只含 x86_64 架构，需要 macOS 26 或更新版本。只在 Apple 芯片
的 Rosetta 下验过，没有在 Intel Mac 上验过。版本号在 App 的「设置 › 帮助」里。

Apple 芯片的 Mac 必须用 Apple 芯片版。在那上面打开这个 Intel 版，系统可能提示对 Intel 应用的支持即将结束。

安装：把 Dayside.app 拖进「应用程序」。

这一版是 ad-hoc 签名、未经公证，第一次打开会被系统拦下。打开方法：到「系统设置 ›
隐私与安全性」拉到最下面，点「仍要打开」，输入密码确认。

数据只存在这台 Mac 上，没有遥测，也不需要账号。日历与通讯录只在你允许后才读取。
